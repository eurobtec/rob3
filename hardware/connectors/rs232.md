By using a CMOS inverter instead of a dedicated MAX232 chip, the designer created a highly cost-effective, passive RS232-to-TTL receiver stage.

## Signal Directions & Operation
- **Top Line (Receive):** The PC sends data out from DB9 Pin 2. It drops across the 10k/100k safety network, enters input Pin 13 of the MM74C04N CMOS inverter, is flipped to safe 5V TTL logic, and leaves Pin 12 to enter the 8031 RXD Pin 10.
- **Bottom Line (Transmit):** The 8031 TXD Pin 11 sends out 0–5V serial data. It passes through a 100k current-limiting resistor into the inverting input Pin 2 of the M34004 Op-Amp. Powered by ±9V, the op-amp boosts and flips this signal out of Pin 1 straight to DB9 Pin 3.
